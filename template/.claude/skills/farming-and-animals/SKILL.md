# Crops

- Wheat grows from wheat seeds and beetroot from beetroot seeds. Carrots and potatoes are planted as they are.
- Farmland is made by tilling dirt or grass with a hoe. The reasons tilling can fail are in the play skill, under Tilling.
- Farmland stays moist with water within 4 blocks, or in the rain with open sky above it. It needs a clear block above.
- Crops need light to grow.
- Crops grow through stages over several in-game days.
- Landing on farmland after a jump or a fall can trample it. Walking does not.
- A grown plant is harvested by breaking it.
- Breaking grass sometimes drops wheat seeds.
- Bone meal speeds growth.

## Animals

- Cows and sheep breed with wheat. Pigs breed with carrots, potatoes or beetroot. Chickens breed with seeds (wheat, beetroot, melon or pumpkin seeds).
- Breeding needs two animals of the same kind, each fed that food. A baby appears and takes a while to grow up.
